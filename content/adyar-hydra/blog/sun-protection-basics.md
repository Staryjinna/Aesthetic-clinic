Skin with more natural pigment burns less easily, but it is still affected by sun. Ultraviolet exposure can deepen pigmentation and contribute to uneven tone over time.

## Practical habits

- Apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning, including on cloudy days
- Use about two finger-lengths for the face and neck
- Reapply after a few hours outdoors
- Add shade, a hat or sunglasses when you can

## Choosing a sunscreen

Look for broad-spectrum protection and a texture you will actually wear daily. Gel and fluid formulas are often comfortable in humid weather.

## If pigmentation is a concern

Persistent dark patches are worth having examined, as they can have different causes that need different approaches.
