Dermal fillers are injectable gels used to restore volume or soften lines. Before deciding, it helps to ask a few plain questions.

## Questions for your doctor

1. Who will perform the treatment, and what are their qualifications?
2. Which product is used, and how long does it usually last?
3. What are the possible side effects, and what happens if something goes wrong?
4. How will the result be balanced with the rest of my face?
5. Is a smaller, more gradual approach an option?

## After treatment

Some swelling or bruising is common and generally settles within days. Follow the aftercare advice you are given and contact the clinic if anything worries you.

*Results vary from person to person. This article is general information, not medical advice.*
