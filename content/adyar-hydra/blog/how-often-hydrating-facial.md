Hydrating facials cleanse, exfoliate and add moisture in a single visit. How often suits you depends on your skin and your goals.

## A common starting point

Many people have a facial every four to six weeks, which roughly matches the skin's natural renewal cycle. Your therapist or doctor may suggest a different rhythm after looking at your skin.

## Things that change the answer

- Sensitive or reactive skin may need longer gaps
- Dry climates and air-conditioned offices can raise hydration needs
- Using retinoids or exfoliating acids at home may change what is suitable

## Between visits

A gentle cleanser, moisturiser and daily sunscreen do much of the work. Facials support a routine rather than replace one.

*Results vary from person to person. This article is general information, not medical advice.*
