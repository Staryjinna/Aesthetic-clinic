Microneedling uses fine needles to make very small, controlled channels in the top layers of skin. The skin responds by starting its natural repair process.

## What it is commonly used for

- Uneven skin texture
- Acne marks and some types of scarring
- Enlarged-looking pores
- General skin quality, sometimes alongside topical serums

## What a session is like

Numbing cream is applied first. The device is then passed over the treated area, with needle depth adjusted for each part of the face. Sessions usually take about an hour including preparation.

## After the session

Redness, mild swelling and a feeling of tightness are common and usually settle within a few days. Daily sun protection is important, and your doctor will give specific aftercare instructions.

## Who may need to wait

Active skin infections, some skin conditions, certain medicines and pregnancy can all affect suitability. That is one reason a consultation comes first.

*Results vary from person to person, and more than one session is often discussed. This article is general information, not medical advice.*
