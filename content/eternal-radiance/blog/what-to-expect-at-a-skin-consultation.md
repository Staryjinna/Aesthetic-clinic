A good aesthetic plan starts with a conversation, not a procedure. A consultation gives you and your doctor time to understand your concerns before any treatment is suggested.

## What the doctor will ask

- What bothers you about your skin, hair or appearance, and for how long
- Your medical history, current medicines and any allergies
- Previous treatments and how your skin responded
- Your daily routine, sun exposure and skincare products

## What happens during the visit

Your skin or scalp is examined closely. The doctor explains what is likely to help, what is unlikely to help, and the options available, including options that do not involve a procedure.

## Questions worth asking

1. What are the possible side effects and how are they managed?
2. How many sessions might be needed, and how will progress be reviewed?
3. What aftercare is required, and is there any downtime?
4. What are the alternatives?

## Before you come

Bring a list of the products you use and any earlier reports. Avoid new skincare products in the days before, so your skin can be assessed as it normally is.

*Results vary from person to person. This article is general information and not a substitute for medical advice.*
