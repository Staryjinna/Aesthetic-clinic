Hair thinning is common and has many different causes. Treating it well starts with working out which cause applies.

## Common contributors

- Pattern (hereditary) hair loss
- Nutritional gaps, including low iron or vitamin D
- Thyroid and hormonal conditions
- Stress, illness or major life events
- Scalp conditions such as dandruff or inflammation

## What an assessment may include

A look at your scalp and hair density, your medical and family history, and sometimes blood tests. Photographs may be taken so changes can be compared over time.

## Options your doctor may discuss

Depending on the cause, options range from correcting an underlying issue, to medicines, to in-clinic therapies, to surgical hair restoration for suitable candidates. Hair growth is gradual, so progress is judged over months rather than weeks.

## When to seek advice

Sudden or patchy hair loss, or thinning with scalp pain or itching, deserves prompt medical attention.

*Results vary from person to person. This article is general information, not medical advice.*
